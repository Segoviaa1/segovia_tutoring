DS

I'm a graduate student in Physical Oceanography at MIT-WHOI, and I've been tutoring math and physics for eight years — long enough to have taught nearly every way a student can get stuck on a quadratic equation or a free-body diagram, and to have found what actually unsticks them.

My own path ran through the same subjects I teach: a B.S. in Physics and Mathematics from Boston College, where I graduated with departmental honors, followed by graduate research applying that math to the physics of the ocean. I tutor because I like watching that same shift happen in someone else — the moment a subject stops feeling like a wall and starts feeling like a tool.
