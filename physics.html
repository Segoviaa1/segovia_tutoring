AP Physics C
Calculus-based — Mechanics and E&M. The full sequence, including the calculus that trips students up: variable acceleration, work as an integral, moments of inertia, Gauss's law, and RC circuits. This is the course I'm most often asked for and the one fewest tutors will take.
Grades 11–12
Honors & Conceptual
First-year physics. Motion, forces, energy, momentum, waves and sound, and introductory electricity. Good for building the intuition that makes an AP course manageable a year later — or just getting through a demanding class with confidence intact.
Grades 9–12

On AP exam timing

AP exams land in the first two weeks of May. Students who start in the fall have room to build real understanding; students who start in March are doing triage. Both are worth doing — but if the exam matters, earlier is worth far more than more hours later. If you're reading this in the fall, this is the cheap moment.
